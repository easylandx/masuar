The area

Mojácar and the Almería coast

Mojácar is one of the most unique villages on the Mediterranean: whitewashed architecture on a hilltop, steps from a protected coastline, with more than 300 days of sunshine a year. The Macenas area and the Mojácar coastline attract the highest tourist and investor demand in the province.

Masuar was born here and knows every corner: which properties sell fastest, what buyer profile is looking in each area, and the real vacation rental potential of each location.
